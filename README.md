I am also a member of the [MATHERIALS](https://www.inria.fr/fr/matherials) team at [Inria Paris](https://www.inria.fr/).

# PhD research

My research focuses on the development of model order reduction methods for nonlinear parametric variational inequalities, with applications motivated in particular by simulations arising in CO2 storage.

I am interested in combining projection-based model reduction, neural networks and hyper-reduction techniques to construct accurate and computationally efficient reduced-order models. My current work includes Proper Orthogonal Decomposition (POD), neural-network-augmented reduced models, hyper-reduction and semi-smooth Newton methods.
