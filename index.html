About

I am a third-year PhD student in applied mathematics at CERMICS, École nationale des ponts et chaussées, in collaboration with IFP Energies nouvelles (IFPEN). I am supervised by Virginie Ehrlacher (CERMICS) and Guillaume Enchéry (IFPEN), and I am a member of the MATHERIALS team at Inria Paris.

My research focuses on model order reduction for nonlinear parametric variational inequalities, motivated by the simulation of CO2 storage in porous media with faults. I combine projection-based reduction, neural networks and hyper-reduction to build reduced-order models that stay accurate while being much cheaper to evaluate.

Research interests

  • Model order reduction for nonlinear parametric problems
  • Variational inequalities and semi-smooth Newton methods
  • Hyper-reduction
  • Scientific machine learning: neural-network-augmented reduced models, neural PDE solvers
