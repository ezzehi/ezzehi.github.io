Student projects

First-year research projects that I proposed and supervised.

  1. Feb – May 2026

    Neural networks for solving partial differential equations 12 h

    Students implemented neural networks in Python, used them to solve 1D and 2D PDEs with physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) and the Deep Ritz method, and compared the results with classical methods.

  2. Feb – May 2026

    Model order reduction: POD and hyper-reduction (DEIM) 12 h

    Finite element solution of simple parametric PDEs (variational formulation, Lax–Milgram, Galerkin method), then hyper-reduced reduced-order models with POD and DEIM, implemented in Python.
